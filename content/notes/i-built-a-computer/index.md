I promised myself to keep my [next video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KNy8R3f58Ms) simple.

So I built a computer.

A 6502 and a homemade GPIO peripheral. It's a follow-up to my memory-mapped I/O video, this time showing what happens after we write to memory.

While sanity-checking the boards, I had Claude write a simple terminal program that simulates them on my computer, to compare it with the real thing one clock at a time. Then I got ambitious and asked it to turn that into a web version. It came out good enough to make me wonder why I even built the boards in the first place.

Click CLOCK, watch the LEDs, and follow along in the program below.
