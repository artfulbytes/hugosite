Is it the software or the hardware?

A question I ask myself when something stops working.

That's when I thank past me for the feral *git commit -am "IT WORKS" *I made an hour ago.

Not because restoring to that state always works. The hardware can be the real problem. But it often helps me rule out the software.

Sometimes I forget, usually when I'm deep into something, tired, just gonna try this one more thing.

A kind reminder.
